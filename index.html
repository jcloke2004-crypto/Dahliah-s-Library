Flora

Arblington PalmCommon

Coastal · Flora · Common · Medicinal

Tall, smooth-trunked palms with enormous fan-shaped leaves lining every street in Arblington. Fruit is oval, amber-colored, with a rich sweet flesh.

Uses

Fruit is nutritious and keeps well on sea voyages; a staple of southern trade ships. Trunk wood produces exceptional charcoal prized by alchemists.

Medicinal

Palm fruit pulp treats scurvy and malnutrition. Juice of the green unripe fruit is mildly antibacterial, used to clean wounds.

Ashpetal LilyUncommon

Mountains & Forest · Flora · Uncommon · Medicinal

Tall lily with grey-tinged white petals and a deep orange center. Grows in ash-enriched soil around Emberglade and Mithrendor. Petals slightly rough, like fine sandpaper.

Uses

A common offering flower at funeral rites in the northeast. Pollen produces a powerful coagulant used in surgery.

Medicinal

Pollen-based coagulant stops severe internal bleeding. One of the most important surgical reagents in Northeast medicine.

Darkrest · Flora · Common

Uses

Timber for construction. Sap (willow tears) mixed with charcoal makes shadow ink for ritual writing and warding glyphs.

BlackmossUncommon

Darkrest · Flora · Uncommon

Dense ink-dark moss with a velvety surface that swallows nearby light. Found on stone in near-total darkness in Drexton's underground ruins.

Uses

Prized reagent for shadow magic and concealment workings. Pressed into candles, it produces a flame that gives no visible light but warms the room.

Blackstone NettleUncommon

Grasslands & Plains · Flora · Uncommon · Medicinal · Toxic

Dark-stemmed nettle with almost black-green leaves edged in tiny white hairs. The sting site turns briefly numb then burns for hours. Grows along the walls of Blackstone.

Uses

Muscle relaxant, anti-seizure. The isolated stinging agent is a localized anesthetic. Excess can cause full-body paralysis.

Bloodtide MossRare

Coastal · Flora · Rare · Medicinal

A deep red-purple moss growing only on rocks submerged at low tide and exposed at high. Looks like dark blood spreading across rock. Smells sharply of salt and copper.

Uses

Extremely valuable to apothecaries. The extracted compound is the most effective broad-spectrum antibiotic available without magical intervention.

Medicinal

Antibiotic, anti-infection. The most powerful natural antibiotic in the world treats infections that resist all other remedies.

BloodwortUncommon

Grasslands & Plains · Flora · Uncommon · Medicinal

Stubby herb with broad dark-red leaves looking like dried blood splattered across green. Grows near water sources. Roots ooze thick crimson sap when cut.

Uses

Root sap is the most commonly used styptic in Heartland's field medicine, slowing bleeding dramatically. Standard in soldier kits.

Medicinal

Static, wound closure, and reduced clotting time. High doses carry mild stimulant properties.

Cinder CapCommon

Mountains & Forest · Flora · Common · Toxic · Medicinal

Charcoal-black mushroom with a flat, wide cap and rough ash-dusted surface. Grows in clusters in volcanic soil around Cinderforge and Mithrendor. When dried, it becomes brittle and flammable.

Uses

Used as a forge-starter; a handful catches flame instantly. Forgemasters harvest them in bulk.

Medicinal

Accidentally eating it causes severe internal burning; there is no antidote.

Medicinal

The paste also treats severe burns; the anti-inflammatory compounds are exceptional.

Uses

Root dried and ground is the base for the most common painkiller in the southern reaches. Also a key ingredient in charm-resistance potions.

Medicinal

Pain management, charm resistance. High doses: powerful hallucinogen. Very high doses: fatal.

Medicinal

Last resort in trauma surgeries, the derived compound can restart a stopped heart once, and once only.

Uses

You do not use an Elder. You hope it does not notice you. House Vaelith's position in Rivendell exists partly because of a centuries-old non-aggression accord with the one in the high Drakthar peaks.

Documented near Alexandria once every generation. Believed to feed on dream states and memories. Temple scholars do not recommend engagement.

Not hunted; attempts are fatal. Shed scales found on Satori banks are used in the highest-tier protective magical items. A single scale is worth a fortune.

Thornback BoarCommon

Grasslands & Plains · Fauna · Common · Medium Danger

Massive wild boar with a prominent ridge of bony horn-like protrusions along the spine. Dark bristled coat, small red eyes. Tusks curve dramatically upward and inward.

Uses

Hunted for meat, tusks, and hide. The bony ridge-plates were used as improvised armor by poorer soldiers.

Thornwing WaspCommon

Grasslands & Plains · Fauna · Common · Medium Danger · Medicinal

Large wasp — three inches long — with overlapping thorn-like plates on its abdomen. Metallic dark green body. Aggressive nest defenders; passive when solitary.

Uses

Venom harvested from nests is the base ingredient for paralytic and surgical agents.

Medicinal

Venom, heavily diluted: paralytic for surgical procedures. Trace amounts reduce inflammation rapidly.

Void StagLegendary

Darkrest · Fauna · Legendary · Low Danger

An enormous stag with entirely black fur, hooves, antlers, and eyes. The antlers seem to absorb light. Walking through the mist, it disappears completely. One per territory; solitary.

Uses

Never successfully hunted. Shed antler fragments used in highest-tier shadow magic rituals. Considered sacred by Umbris shamans.

Wraith EelUncommon

Coastal · Fauna · Uncommon · High Danger · Medicinal

Translucent, ghost-like eels three to six feet long that phase partially through solid matter. Their outline is blurred at the edges. The electric discharge is silent and blue-white.

Uses

Wraith Eel venom is the most potent known hallucinogen. Victims experience their deepest fears made real for hours.

Medicinal

In vanishingly small doses: treats severe repressed trauma by giving the patient controlled access to buried memories. Extremely specialized and dangerous.
