Flora

Arblington PalmCommon

Common · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine

Tall, smooth-trunked palms with enormous fan-shaped leaves lining every street in Arblington. Fruit is oval, amber-colored, with a rich sweet flesh.

Uses

Fruit is nutritious and keeps well on sea voyages — a staple of southern trade ships. Trunk wood produces exceptional charcoal prized by alchemists.

Medicinal

Palm fruit pulp treats scurvy and malnutrition. Juice of the green unripe fruit is mildly antibacterial — used to clean wounds.

Ashpetal LilyUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Tall lily with grey-tinged white petals and a deep orange center. Grows in ash-enriched soil around Emberglade and Mithrendor. Petals slightly rough, like fine sandpaper.

Uses

A common offering flower at funeral rites in the northeast. Pollen produces a powerful coagulant used in surgery.

Medicinal

Pollen-based coagulant stops severe internal bleeding. One of the most important surgical reagents in northeast medicine.

AshwillowCommon

Common · Flora · Darkrest

Tall, pale-barked trees with cascading silver-black leaves that never shed. Bark is smooth and cold, almost like chilled bone. Branches droop low, trailing across the ground in fog.

Uses

Timber for basic construction. The sap (willow-tears) mixed with charcoal makes shadow ink used in ritual writing and warding glyphs.

BlackmossUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Darkrest

Dense, ink-dark moss with a velvety surface that swallows the light around it. Found on stone in near-total darkness throughout Drexton's underground ruins.

Uses

Prized reagent for shadow magic and concealment workings. Pressed into candles it produces a flame that gives no visible light but warms the room.

Blackstone NettleUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine · Toxic

Dark-stemmed nettle with almost black-green leaves edged in tiny white hairs. Sting site turns briefly numb then burns for hours. Grows along the walls of Blackstone.

Uses

Used in controlled doses to treat muscle spasm disorders and seizures.

Medicinal

Muscle relaxant, anti-seizure. Stinging agent isolated is a localized anesthetic. Dangerous in excess — can cause full-body paralysis.

Bloodroot CreeperUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Forest · Medium Danger · Medicine

Deep-red vine that actively grows toward the heat signature of warm-blooded creatures. Thorns secrete an anticoagulant that prevents wounds from clotting. Found in forest.

Uses

Anticoagulant used in surgeries requiring controlled blood flow. Can be trained away from settlements if managed carefully.

Bloodtide MossRare

Rare · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine

A deep red-purple moss growing only on rocks submerged at low tide and exposed at high. Looks like dark blood spreading across rock. Smells sharply of salt and copper.

Uses

Extremely valuable to apothecaries. The extracted compound is the most effective broad-spectrum antibiotic available without magical intervention.

Medicinal

Antibiotic, anti-infection. The most powerful natural antibiotic in the world — treats infections that resist all other remedies.

BloodwortUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Stubby herb with broad dark-red leaves looking like dried blood splattered across green. Grows near water sources. Roots ooze thick crimson sap when cut.

Uses

Root sap is the most commonly used styptic in Heartlands field medicine. Slows bleeding dramatically. Standard in soldier kits.

Medicinal

Styptic, wound closure, reduces clotting time. High doses carry mild stimulant properties.

Cinder CapCommon

Common · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine · Toxic

Charcoal-black mushroom with a flat, wide cap and rough ash-dusted surface. Grows in clusters in volcanic soil around Cinderforge and Mithrendor. Dried, becomes brittle and flammable.

Uses

Used as a forge-starter — a handful catches flame instantly. Forgemasters harvest them in bulk.

Medicinal

Accidentally eaten causes severe internal burning — no antidote.

Coldthorn BriarCommon

Common · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine

Low sprawling shrubs with dark crystalline-looking thorns and small dark-red berries in autumn.

Uses

Berries mildly toxic raw. Fermented they produce Coldthorn Mead — a fiery warming spirit common in Opal Village. Thorns used in trap-making.

Medicinal

Fermented berry juice, heavily diluted, treats circulation failure and hypothermia.

Deepwater KelpCommon

Common · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine

Massive kelp forests in the waters around Deepwater Haven. Individual strands reach sixty feet. The fronds produce bioluminescent bubbles at night, making the harbor glow blue-green.

Uses

Dried and burned as incense in sea-blessing rituals. Fresh kelp is a dietary staple along the coast.

Medicinal

Kelp tea is a broad-spectrum nutritional supplement. The iodine-analog compound treats thyroid-adjacent conditions.

Dragonfire PepperUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine · Toxic

Bright orange-red pepper plant growing near volcanic soil. The peppers pulse faintly with heat — warm to the touch even after picking. Cutting one releases a cloud of burning vapor.

Uses

A critical spice in House Vaelith's trade catalog. Extremely painful to handle without gloves.

Medicinal

Compounds treat circulation failure and clear blocked airways. Overdose causes internal burns.

Dragonscale FernUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Wide, low fern with leathery fronds segmented in overlapping sections genuinely resembling scales. Color ranges green to bronze near active dragon nesting sites.

Uses

Ground fronds produce a paste conferring temporary fire resistance. Used by smiths and Drakthar dragon riders as standard protection.

Medicinal

The paste also treats severe burns — the anti-inflammatory compounds are exceptional.

Duskbell VineUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Climbing vine with small dusty-blue bell-shaped flowers that open only at dusk and close at sunrise. Drapes across old walls throughout Alexandria and Rosemoore.

Uses

Flowers steeped in warm milk make a well-known sleep aid. Component in folk love-magic.

Medicinal

Mild sedative. Safe for children. Treats insomnia and restless sleep.

Emberthorn TreeCommon

Common · Flora · Cinderforge

Massive trees with bark that glows faint orange-red at night as if lit from within. Fire-resistant, dark-grained wood. Leaves are broad, waxy, deep red-green. Radiates faint warmth.

Uses

Primary construction wood of Cinderforge. Holds enchantments extremely well, especially fire and metalworking magic.

Fiore BloomCommon

Common · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine

Large bright flower with vivid yellow petals edged in deep red, growing on low sprawling plants across the Fiore coastal plains. Stamens dense and orange. Scent is pungent and spicy.

Uses

Dried stamens are Fiore Spice — House Vaelith's most profitable trade good in northern markets. Petals edible and mildly spiced.

Medicinal

Fiore pollen dissolved in water treats respiratory congestion and certain allergic reactions.

Forestwarden TreeRare

Rare · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Ancient, impossibly large trees in deepest Faenoria forest. Trunks take twenty people to encircle. Bark grows in geometric patterns suggesting intelligence. Said to be over 2,000 years old.

Uses

The wood cannot be cut — any blade touching it breaks. Elven communities build around and into them. Indicates powerful ley-line convergence.

Medicinal

The sap weeping from natural wounds is a legendary-tier healing agent — so rare as to be near-mythic.

FrostbloomUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine

A small, six-petaled flower in deep violet shading to white at the tips. Waxy petals with a faint crystalline sheen. Blooms only in sub-zero temperatures; freezes solid if brought into warmth.

Uses

Crushed petals in a paste dull severe pain and induce calm. Dried petals in teas ease nerve damage from cold exposure.

Medicinal

Pain relief, frostbite treatment, mild sedative. Overuse causes dependency and emotional blunting.

Goldvein GrassCommon

Common · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Tall waving grass with a golden thread running through each blade, catching sunlight like fine wire. Fields shimmer in the wind.

Uses

Woven into lucky baskets. Bundles burned in hearths ward malicious spirits. Used in estate ceremonies throughout the Heartlands.

Medicinal

Infused in oil it soothes skin rashes and minor burns.

Grimcap MushroomCommon

Common · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine · Toxic

Squat, dark-grey mushroom with a cracked concave cap and pitch-black underside. Grows on dead wood in Drexton's older districts. Smells of ash.

Uses

Highly toxic raw — causes paralysis of the lungs within hours. In tiny doses only: a powerful sleep aid.

Medicinal

Microscopically dosed tinctures treat severe insomnia. Extremely dangerous to prepare.

HeartbloomRare

Rare · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Single large flower on a long stem, deep red at center fading to pale pink. Found only in undisturbed meadows far from roads. The bloom always faces the nearest living creature.

Uses

A profound romantic symbol in Heartlands culture. Used in high-tier healing magic to accelerate recovery from near-fatal wounds.

Medicinal

Heart-stabilizing tonic, accelerates recovery from severe blood loss. One of the most potent natural healing reagents available.

Hollowroot MushroomUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine · Toxic

Pale cream mushroom with a broad upturned cap and hollow interior stem. Grows in perfect circles near Hallow's cemetery district. Gills shimmer faintly in darkness.

Uses

Controlled by Hallow's civic authorities. Consumed, produces powerful visions believed to be glimpses of the spirit world.

Medicinal

Microdose treats persistent grief-induced catatonia. Overdose causes irreversible hallucinatory psychosis.

Icevein FernRare

Rare · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine

Pale blue-green fronds with translucent veining carrying a faint internal glow in deep cold. Found only in frost-pocket caves near Darkrest's highest peaks.

Uses

Vein fluid is the primary ingredient in a high-value anticoagulant used by surgeons. Also used in cold-resistance enchanting ink.

Medicinal

Anticoagulant, wound packing to prevent tissue freezing in extreme cold. Rare and expensive.

Ironbark OakCommon

Common · Flora · Rosemoore

Massive trees with bark the color and texture of hammered dark iron. Broad deep green leaves. Trunk sparks when struck with a normal axe.

Uses

Used in shipbuilding and fortress construction. Holds enchantments exceptionally well — most enchanted doors in Heartlands castles are Ironbark.

IronbloomUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Small, hardy flower with petals of deep metallic blue-grey and a golden center. Grows in rocky soil of Mithrendor and forge sites. The stem springs back upright when stepped on.

Uses

Metallic compound in petals, extracted alchemically, produces a hardening agent for metals. Smiths mix trace amounts into high-quality alloys.

Medicinal

Petals steeped as tea treat iron-deficiency anemia. The metallic compound absorbs into the bloodstream remarkably efficiently.

Marinwood MangrakeUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine · Toxic

Thick-rooted coastal plant near Marinwood with roots forking in a rough humanoid shape. Broad, waxy dark-green leaves. Pulling a Mangrake root from the ground produces a high-pitched whine.

Uses

Root dried and ground is the base for the most common painkiller in the southern reaches. Also a key ingredient in charm-resistance potions.

Medicinal

Pain management, charm resistance. High doses: powerful hallucinogen. Very high doses: fatal.

Mimic FungusRare

Rare · Flora · Forest · High Danger · Toxic

Colony fungus in the forest that mimics the appearance of valuable herbs or mushrooms. When harvested, releases a cloud of highly toxic spores.

Uses

Spores safely contained are a potent poison ingredient. Underground mass provides rare alchemical substrate.

Moonveil OrchidRare

Rare · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Luminous orchid with pale silver-white petals emitting soft light mimicking moonlight. The glow intensifies on the full moon. Found only in deep Faenoria forest glades.

Uses

Sacred to Faenoria's elven communities — harvesting uninvited is a serious offense. On the black market a single fresh bloom commands extraordinary prices.

Medicinal

The light emitted has measurable healing properties for eye ailments and light-deprivation illness.

NightbellLegendary

Legendary · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine

Tiny jet-black bell-shaped flower with faint silver luminescence at midnight only. Grows in clusters of exactly three, never more. Extraordinarily rare.

Uses

Rarest alchemical ingredient in the region. Pollen brews a tonic suppressing a vampire's bloodlust for days.

Medicinal

Bloodlust suppression, high-tier sleep inducement, soul-anchoring rituals. Priceless.

Rivendell IvyCommon

Common · Flora · Cinderforge · Medicine

Thick climbing ivy with bright silver-green leaves with an almost metallic quality. Old growth develops stems as thick as a wrist. Smells of cedar and something sweeter underneath.

Uses

Cultivated as a status symbol by noble estates in Rivendell. House Vaelith's walls are renowned for their coverage.

Medicinal

Fresh crushed leaves in a compress reduce fever and treat mild respiratory infections.

Rockmaw PlantUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Mountains · Medium Danger

Grows in mountain rocky crevices disguised as a natural crack in stone. Inner surfaces lined with backward-pointing mineral spines and a strong adhesive sap.

Uses

Adhesive sap is the strongest natural bonding agent known. Mineral spines used in specialist drilling and carving tools.

Rosemoore RoseCommon

Common · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Deep almost black-red rose on thorned canes. Blooms are large, dense, heavily fragrant — sweet with an undertone of iron. Thorns curve inward.

Uses

Perfumes, dyes, culinary garnish. Petals in wine make a mild charming tonic. Roots used in compulsion-adjacent potions.

Medicinal

Rose hip tea treats infections and vitamin deficiency. Petal compress reduces swelling.

Saltbloom CactusUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine

A barrel cactus in the arid parts of the Fiore coast. Spines are white and hollow. In the dry season it produces vivid purple flowers almost too bright to look at directly.

Uses

Hollow spines used as needles and fine tools. The core holds a large reservoir of clean water — essential survival knowledge in the dry south.

Medicinal

The cactus water has natural electrolyte compounds — standard treatment for dehydration and heat sickness along southern trade routes.

Satori DeathbellRare

Rare · Flora · Deepwater Haven · Medicine · Toxic

A pale, waxy flower shaped like a funeral bell on long black stems from Satori swamp mud. Always found in pairs — never singular. The scent is faintly sweet, masking something else entirely.

Uses

Used in death-rite ceremonies. On the black market: a component in the most powerful undetectable poisons known.

Medicinal

Extreme palliative care — the extract eases the passage of the actively dying, removing pain entirely in the final hours.

Satori MangroveCommon

Common · Flora · Deepwater Haven

Twisted pale-white mangroves in the Swamps of Satori with roots forming labyrinthine cages above the water. Bark is smooth and bone-white. No birds nest in them. In fog they look like reaching hands.

Uses

The pale wood, when burned, produces smoke that thins the barrier between living and dead — used in Satori death-rituals. Roots harvested for weaving — strong and rot-resistant.

SilverleafRare

Rare · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Delicate tree with leaves silver on the underside, reflecting light brightly. Easy to spot from a distance when the wind turns them. Found in undisturbed woodland south of Alexandria.

Uses

Wood used in high-value warding work — the reflected-light property magically deflects minor enchantments.

Medicinal

Primary ingredient in anti-poison antidotes for contact toxins. Extremely valuable.

Siren WeedUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Coastal · High Danger

Bright red aquatic plant growing in dense mats in coastal shallows. Releases sweet-smelling euphoric vapour above the waterline. Draws creatures into the water where tendrils entangle them.

Uses

Euphoric compound extracted and stabilised has legitimate uses as an anxiety treatment. Harvesting requires breathing protection.

Snapjaw BloomUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Forest · Medium Danger

Large carnivorous flower with hinged jaw-like petals lined with inward-pointing teeth. Attracts prey with a sweet heavy scent. Snaps shut instantly on contact. Found in forest.

Uses

Digestive fluid treats infected wounds when diluted. Teeth used as natural fish hooks and cutting tools.

Snowcap ShroudUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Frozen Lands · Medium Danger

Flat carnivorous plant that lies under a thin layer of snow, indistinguishable from the surface. When stepped on, folds upward, sealing around a limb and secreting a paralysing cold compound.

Uses

Paralytic secretion used in cold-climate surgical procedures and high-end poison crafting.

Sorrow WillowRare

Rare · Flora · Darkrest

Ancient cousin of the Ashwillow with split, scarred bark and dark red-brown trailing branches that sway without wind. Found in Umbris City's oldest districts.

Uses

Ground bark produces a deep dye for mourning garments. Considered deeply unlucky to cut. Strongly associated with vampiric bloodlines.

Starweave MossLegendary

Legendary · Flora · Cinderforge

Luminous moss found in a single known location — a deep cavern beneath Faenoria forest. Each spore glows a different color, creating a shifting tapestry mapping the stars as they were 1,000 years ago.

Uses

Cannot be harvested without destroying it — touching it causes the light to fade permanently. Elven scholars visit it as a pilgrimage.

Sunweaver VineLegendary

Legendary · Flora · Deepwater Haven

Reported once per generation along the Fiore coast — a vine of pure golden color with leaves catching and redirecting sunlight into geometric patterns on the ground. Always found growing toward the sea.

Uses

The location is considered sacred land. The vine itself cannot be harvested — attempts result in it wilting immediately to nothing.

Swamp Whisper ReedCommon

Common · Flora · Deepwater Haven

Tall grey-green reeds in dense thickets in the Satori shallows. The hollow stems in wind produce sounds indistinguishable from whispered voices.

Uses

Used to make pipes and musical instruments with an eerily vocal quality. Bundles kept in homes are believed to carry the voices of ancestors.

The GloommawRare

Rare · Flora · Cinderforge · Medium Danger · Medicine

A pit-like plant covered in slimy amber-green mucus with vines that extend outward along the ashen ground, contracting to drag surrounding leaves and debris inward to trap prey. Found in the volcanic soil margins of Cinderforge.

Uses

Produces a highly fertile soil as a waste byproduct — unusually valuable in Cinderforge's ash-heavy terrain.

Medicinal

The saliva coating the inner walls, if collected carefully, can treat and repair magical burns. It also carries mild contraceptive properties when properly diluted and prepared by an apothecary.

Thornwhip VineUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Grasslands · Medium Danger

Lies flat across grassland ground. Launches thorned whip-tendrils to snare prey around the ankles and drag it inward. Found across open plains.

Uses

Thorns used in poison-delivery tools. Root sap is a mild paralytic when diluted.

Traveler's MintCommon

Common · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine

Low-growing mint with dark green leaves and tiny white flowers. Found along every road in the Heartlands. Strong, sharp, cooling scent.

Uses

A ubiquitous roadside herb. Used in cooking, teas, and as a natural insect repellent. Every tavern between Rosemoore and Alexandria keeps it on hand.

Medicinal

Digestive aid, headache relief, reduces fever. Used by field medics for nausea. A strong daily infusion is widely used among women as a mild contraceptive — the practice is so common and openly known it is considered everyday knowledge along the Heartlands roads.

VeilweedUncommon

Uncommon · Flora · Darkrest · Medicine

Semi-transparent vine with pale grey-green coloring that becomes nearly invisible when dried. Common on ruins near Asura Village.

Uses

Dried and woven into cloaks that blend into low-light environments. Also used to wrap wounds to reduce scarring.

Medicinal

Pulped fresh vine applied to cuts slows bleeding and numbs the wound edge.

Witch's CapLegendary

Legendary · Flora · Rosemoore · Medicine · Toxic

Stark white mushroom with a perfectly conical cap and no visible gills. Grows alone — never in clusters. Does not decay even when cut. Flesh is cold to the touch always.

Uses

In the right hands: catalyst for resurrection-adjacent rituals. In the wrong hands: an undetectable poison that mimics natural death.

Medicinal

Last resort in trauma surgeries — the derived compound can restart a stopped heart once, and once only.

SliverleafRare

Grasslands & Plains · Flora · Rare · Medicinal

Delicate tree with leaves silver on the underside, reflecting light brightly and easy to spot from a distance when the wind turns them. Found in undisturbed woodland south of Alexandria.

Uses

Wood used in high-value warding and staff work reflects the light property, which magically deflects minor enchantments.

Medicinal

Primary ingredient in anti-poison antidotes for contact toxins. Extremely valuable.

Fauna

Abyssal SharkUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Deep Water · Medium Danger · Hunt

A pale, eyeless shark adapted to lightless depths. Navigates by sensing vibration and electrical fields. Faster than surface sharks. Highly aggressive toward anything that enters its depth range.

Uses

Teeth are exceptionally hard — used in cutting tools and weapon serrations. Cartilage used in joint-repair medicine.

AshfoxCommon

Common · Fauna · Cinderforge · Low Danger · Pet

Small foxes with ashen-grey coats and tails tipped with a flame-like natural glow — warm orange fading to pale white. The glow brightens when excited or alarmed. Amber eyes.

Uses

Kept as household companions. Clever enough to pick simple locks and operate latches. Shed tail-light-hairs are a minor magical reagent.

Bark OwlCommon

Common · Fauna · Forest · Low Danger · Pet

A medium-sized owl with feathers patterned precisely like tree bark — near impossible to spot when perched. Eyes are large and amber. Absolutely silent in flight.

Uses

Trained as a scout familiar and message carrier. Their silence in flight makes them valuable for covert operations. Feathers used in stealth-enchanting.

Borehole WormCommon

Common · Fauna · Mountains · Insect · Low Danger · Hunt

Small worms that bore through rock with a mineralised head-plate. They leave perfectly smooth, circular tunnels and deposit fine mineral dust behind them. Colonies can destabilise loose rock faces.

Uses

The mineralised head-plate is used as a precision drilling bit. The mineral dust they deposit varies by location and is collected for use in compound crafting.

Cavebat ColonyCommon

Common · Fauna · Mountains · Safe · Farmable

Large colonies of mountain bats roosting in cave networks. Individual bats are harmless — a disturbed colony of thousands is a serious hazard.

Uses

Guano is an exceptional fertiliser. Managed colonies are highly productive.

Cliffhorn GoatCommon

Common · Fauna · Mountains · Safe · Farmable · Pet

Hardy mountain goats with long curved horns and a thick, shaggy grey-white coat. They navigate near-vertical rock faces with casual ease. Utterly unbothered by humans.

Uses

Milk is extremely rich and nutritious. Wool is warm and weather-resistant. Easily kept in mountain settlements. The horns, when shed naturally, make excellent tool handles.

Cliffwing PelicanCommon

Common · Fauna · Coastal · Low Danger · Hunt

Enormous pelicans nesting on coastal cliffs. Their pouches can hold a remarkable amount. They dive from incredible heights.

Uses

Feathers used in waterproof bedding and insulation. Eggs are a local delicacy. Some communities train them as fish-delivery animals.

Coldfire MothRare

Rare · Fauna · Darkrest · Low Danger · Medicine

Large moth with deep blue and white patterned wings emitting a soft cold glow. When startled, releases a cloud of glowing scales.

Uses

Glowing scale-dust used in cold-light lanterns producing faint blue luminescence for hours.

Medicinal

Scale powder infused in water has a mild calming effect when inhaled as steam — used for anxiety and panic.

Copperback StagCommon

Common · Fauna · Rosemoore · Low Danger · Medicine

Enormous deer with rust-copper metallic antlers. Warm brown coat with a coppery sheen. Males stand nearly six feet at the shoulder.

Uses

Protected by royal decree — hunted only in sanctioned noble events. Antler velvet used in high-end restorative tonics.

Medicinal

Antler velvet tonic: general recovery accelerant, used in post-battle treatment by noble houses.

CrystalmiteCommon

Common · Fauna · Frozen Lands · Insect · Safe · Hunt

Tiny insects with translucent crystalline shells that refract light beautifully. They cluster on ice formations in the thousands, creating rainbow light shows on bright days.

Uses

The crystalline shell material is used in optical instruments and light-based magical devices. Harvesting requires fine tools and patience.

Dappled DeerCommon

Common · Fauna · Forest · Safe · Hunt · Farmable

Slender forest deer with white-spotted coats that shift to plain grey-brown in winter. Move nearly silently through undergrowth. Alert and cautious but not aggressive.

Uses

Primary forest meat source. Hide used for fine leather. Antlers used in crafting and ritual objects. Can be ranched in large enclosed woodland areas.

Deepcurrent RayCommon

Common · Fauna · Deep Water · Low Danger · Hunt

Enormous flat rays that ride deep-water currents with barely a movement. Wingspan reaches fifteen feet. Their skin generates a mild electric field for navigation. Passive unless stepped on.

Uses

Meat is a deep-sea delicacy. The electric organ is used in magical devices requiring a steady low-level charge.

Deepsinger WhaleUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Deepwater Haven · Low Danger · Medicine

Enormous dark-blue whales making seasonal migrations through Deepwater Haven's sea routes. Their song can be heard on shore on clear nights — a deep resonant sound felt as much as heard.

Uses

Encountering one is considered extraordinary luck by sailors. Their songs carry genuine magical resonance that calms storms in proximity.

Medicinal

Recordings of their songs used in palliative care wards — the resonance measurably reduces patient distress.

Dire WolfUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Grasslands · Forest · Frozen Lands · High Danger · Avoid

Substantially larger than a common wolf — shoulder height reaching a grown person's chest. Grey-black coat with eyes that catch light in a deep amber-red. Hunt in coordinated packs with a level of tactical intelligence that unsettles experienced hunters. A pack will test, retreat, circle, and wait before committing.

Uses

Not recommended for hunting. Pups, extremely rarely, can be raised into formidable companions — but the process is lengthy, dangerous, and not guaranteed. Pelts from those killed defensively are exceptionally thick and warm.

Drifting SealCommon

Common · Fauna · Coastal · Safe · Hunt · Pet

Round, good-natured seals with speckled grey coats. Haul out on coastal rocks in large colonies. Curious about humans and will follow small boats out of interest.

Uses

Hunted for oil used in waterproofing and lamp fuel. Hide makes excellent waterproof leather. Pups occasionally kept as harbour companions.

DuskwolfCommon

Common · Fauna · Darkrest · High Danger

Large wolves with ashen-grey coats that shift toward blue-white in snowfall. Eyes are pale gold, almost white. Built for total silence.

Uses

Pelts are warm and water-resistant. Claws used in ritual jewelry. Some communities have partially domesticated them as guard animals.

Faenoria SpriteUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Cinderforge · Medium Danger · Medicine · Pet

Small, fast-moving fae beings six to ten inches tall with gossamer wings and luminous skin in varying colors — green, blue, gold. Their laughter sounds like wind chimes.

Uses

Mischievous but not malicious — steal small precious objects and lead travelers astray. Elven rangers know their tricks.

Medicinal

Shed wing-dust in suspension treats severe depression. Ethically difficult to source.

Fiore ParrotCommon

Common · Fauna · Deepwater Haven · Low Danger · Pet

Brilliantly colored parrots — every individual a different combination of vivid green, red, blue, and gold. Larger than typical parrots. Their eyes are sharp and evaluating. They watch people.

Uses

They mimic not just speech but emotional intonation and occasionally magical incantations. A parrot that has memorized a spell fragment can trigger minor effects.

Forest BearCommon

Common · Fauna · Forest · Medium Danger · Hunt

A large black-brown bear with patches of moss growing in its thick coat, blending perfectly with forest floor. Generally avoids humans but will attack if its cubs or food supply is threatened.

Uses

Hunted for meat, thick hide, and bear fat used in salves and waterproofing. The moss from its coat has mild healing properties when harvested fresh.

ForgeantUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Cinderforge · Medium Danger · Medicine

Enormous fire-ants — two to three inches long — with bodies the color of heated iron. Mandibles spark on hard surfaces. Colonies of thousands create a shimmering crawling carpet of heat.

Uses

The Cinderforge smiths maintain an uneasy truce — Forgeant colonies clean and purify metals that resist conventional processing.

Medicinal

The acid, isolated and diluted, removes dead tissue from infected wounds when nothing else works.

FrostflyCommon

Common · Fauna · Frozen Lands · Insect · Low Danger

Small flies that survive extreme cold by producing a natural antifreeze compound. Wings are tinged pale blue. Their bite causes a numbing sensation in the affected area.

Uses

The antifreeze compound extracted from their bodies is used in frostbite prevention salves and cold-weather engineering applications.

FrostlynxUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Darkrest · Medium Danger

Large wild cat with a thick white-silver coat, oversized snow-travel paws, and black-tipped tufted ears. Eyes an unsettling pale blue. Hunts by ambush from above near Drexton.

Uses

Fur highly valued for winter cloaks. Claws used in trap-making. Occasionally kept as status animals by Umbris nobility.

Gemwing ButterflyUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Mountains · Insect · Safe · Hunt

Large mountain butterflies with wings that incorporate tiny natural mineral crystals, creating a gem-like iridescent effect. Only found above a certain altitude.

Uses

Wing fragments are used in jewellery, fine art, and enchanting focusing elements. The crystal compound secreted into the wings is a rare natural material with light-focusing properties.

Glacial SerpentUncommon

Uncommon · Fauna · Darkrest · High Danger · Medicine

Enormous pale-white constrictors up to twelve feet long, with frosted-texture scales and a faintly blue-tinted underbelly. Their breath fogs the air even in moderate temperatures.

Uses

Scales used for cold-resistant armor plating. Venom glands produce a freeze agent for food preservation and ice-based alchemy.

A shed fragment from a Void Stag's antler — absorbs light around it. Found in deep wilderness locations.

Uses

A catalyst for shadow magic rituals of the highest tier. Extremely rare — most practitioners never encounter one in a lifetime.

Voidglass ShardRare

Rare · Loot · Magic Stones · Stone

A fragment of perfectly black glass found near deep ley-line convergence points. Light does not reflect off it — it simply disappears into the surface. Cold to the touch regardless of environment.

Uses

A component in shadow magic focusing devices. When set into a weapon, it suppresses magical enchantments it strikes. Highly sought and carefully controlled.

Webwitch Silk SkeinRare

Rare · Loot · Drops

A skein of Webwitch Spider silk, harvested from a killed specimen. Stronger than steel wire at a fraction of the weight.

Uses

Woven into ultra-light armour, climbing equipment, and precision surgical thread. A single skein is worth considerable coin.

White Leviathan ScaleLegendary

Legendary · Loot · Drops

Found on glacier surfaces — shed naturally by the creature. A single plate the size of a shield, perfectly transparent with a faint blue tint.

Uses

Provides full magical cold resistance when worked into armour. The most valuable single-drop material found in frozen biomes.
